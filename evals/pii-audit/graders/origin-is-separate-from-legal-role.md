shipping_address and notes are free-form fields that callers supply and the service stores as-is; customer_email is taken from the request as well.

PASS if the document records, per item or per store, whether it is actively collected or caller-supplied, and records the legal role (controller / processor / not handling) separately, as either decided with a basis or left open pending the statute or counsel.

FAIL if it merges the two (for example "caller-supplied, so not our responsibility") or records only one of them.
